# Apollo.io — Complete Workflow Inventory (what users actually DO)

*Compiled 14 Sep 2026 from Apollo's own Knowledge Base, fetched through its Zendesk API. The whole English KB was pulled — **10 categories, 55 sections, 286 articles** — converted to text and read in full. Companion to [07-apollo-settings-map.md](07-apollo-settings-map.md), which maps Settings; this memo maps the **work**. Not found in the KB = **(unverified)**.*

**Method.** `.../api/v2/help_center/en-us/categories.json` → `.../sections.json` → `.../articles.json?per_page=100&page=N` returns every article with its `body`, bypassing the block on plain page fetches. Search lives at `.../api/v2/help_center/articles/search.json?query=<terms>` — **without** the `en-us` segment; with it the API returns a null count. Article URLs are `https://knowledge.apollo.io/hc/en-us/articles/<id>`, so only the **id** is given.

**What counts as a workflow.** A job with a trigger, steps and an outcome. Where Apollo documents several routes to the same job (add contacts to a sequence from search, list, CSV or profile), that is **one** row with the variants named inside. This takes ~340 documented procedures down to the **234 workflows** below. Nothing is dropped; variants are named.

**Reading the tables.** **Who**: SDR · AE · MK marketer · CS · OPS RevOps/admin · DEV · LDR leader. **Freq**: D daily · W weekly · M monthly · O once (or once per object). **Steps** are Apollo's documented path, compressed; `>` is navigation. **Credits** are quoted from the KB; `—` means no stated cost.

> Apollo publishes no role or frequency data anywhere in its KB. **Who, Freq and Trigger are researcher inference throughout** and are not flagged row by row. Steps, Feeds and Credits are first-party unless marked.

## The credit model, once

Credits are the spine of nearly every workflow, so they are collected here instead of repeated 234 times. All figures quoted from the KB.

| Action | Cost |
|---|---|
| Verified email / net-new contact saved | **1 credit per contact**, however many addresses return. Refunded if an Apollo-verified email bounces within **30 days** |
| Verify an email you edited or uploaded | 1 credit. Uploading or editing is free |
| Job-change update | 1 credit per update; never charged twice for data already bought |
| Mobile or personal phone | Charged **even for DNC-listed numbers**. **No refunds.** HQ, corporate and the office number returned with an email are free |
| Add net-new to a list or sequence | 1 credit per verified email. Already-saved contacts are free |
| Assign an owner to an **unsaved** record | 1 credit per record |
| Waterfall enrichment | Charged **only when a source returns data**; a run that finds nothing is free. A validator charges extra. Email and mobile use **separate balances**. API-key sources bill you directly |
| Enrich a saved contact's email or phone | Charged even if the value is identical. Free for all account fields and for First Name, Last Name, Location, LinkedIn URL, Role Transition |
| Connected call | **30-second minimum**, rounded up in 30-second steps. Ring-out, busy, failed, cancelled = zero |
| AI research | **1 credit per record per column** (100 contacts × 3 columns = 300). **3 free previews per prompt** |
| AI writing assistant | No credits; burns the plan's **monthly AI word limit** (no rollover). **50 generations per person per 24h** |
| AI Assistant chat | Messages free; credits only on credit-consuming actions, and it asks first. Free plans **5 chats/cycle, 10 messages/chat** |
| Email warmup | 1 mailbox per seat on paid plans; each extra **200 credits / 30 days**. Auto-pauses if credits run out |
| Domain purchase | **1,500 credits per domain per year.** Non-refundable |
| Mailbox purchase | SMTP **300**, Google Workspace **800**, Microsoft 365 **1,500** credits per month. Non-refundable |
| Inbox placement tests, blocklist checks, form enrichment | **Free** (placement tests still count against the 24h send total) |
| Apollo API | Create/update/list/manage = **0 credits**. Enrichment, org search, news search, AI-insight endpoints charge **when qualifying data returns**. People Match: **0.01 credits**, 100/min, 300/hour |
| MCP and CLI | Same balance and permissions as the web app. Enrichment and **company search** charge; people search, contact search, sequence create/enrol, one-off email, tasks, analytics are free |
| Your own LLM key | **Bypasses Apollo credits**; billing moves to your provider |

Spend is attributed to a **Surface** — Web App, Automation, Extension, MCP, API, CLI, Uncategorized — but only from **1 Feb 2026**.

## 23. What falls outside the current Ollopa boundary

[PRODUCT.md](../../PRODUCT.md) draws the line like this. **Inside:** contacts and companies, lists and segments, outreach sequences (email, call **tasks**, LinkedIn **tasks**), pipeline and deals, campaigns and audiences, basic reporting, integrations (CRM sync, calendar, email provider, data enrichment, Slack), and agents that research accounts, draft outreach, score leads and propose next steps. **Outside:** paid ads, website builder, support desk, billing of the customer's own customers. The fourteen pages are Home, People, Companies, Lists, Sequences, Inbox, Tasks, Deals, Campaigns, Accounts, Reports, Agents, Settings, Connect an integration — and PRODUCT.md says a dialer, meetings booking, ads, a website builder and a support desk are *"deliberately absent… The app does not hint that they exist."*

Measured against that line, **77 of the 234 workflows above (33%) sit wholly or partly outside Ollopa.** They fall into three groups (A–C), in rough order of how strong the case is for widening — 30 in A, 41 in B, 6 in C. D lists what is inside the boundary but thinner in Ollopa than in Apollo, and E the decisions this forces.

### A. Already implied by the boundary but not yet built — cheap to admit

| Area | Workflows affected | Why it's arguably already inside |
|---|---|---|
| **Data enrichment as a place** | 16 (§4) | PRODUCT.md lists "data enrichment" as an integration, but Apollo treats it as a whole surface: a Data Health Center, scheduled jobs, waterfall provider order, CSV enrichment, job-change alerts. The settings inventory already contains "enrichment provider order", so the *setting* is inside while the *work* is not. **This is the largest single gap and the one most likely to be an oversight rather than a decision.** |
| **Credits and usage** | 3 (§22) | The settings inventory marks "credit balance and burn rate" decision-critical, and Ollopa's agents have per-run and monthly credit caps — but there is no page where a user investigates *where the credits went*. Apollo's Credit usage screen (by feature, action, team member and **surface**) is the missing half. |
| **Scoring, signals and buying intent** | 6 (§6) | Agents that "score leads" are inside the boundary, and the settings inventory has no scoring model. Apollo's auto-scores, custom scores, signals and intent topics are the configuration those agents would need. |
| **Forms and inbound capture** | 5 (§5) | Campaigns and audiences are inside; a form is how an audience gets built from inbound. Apollo's Form Builder and form enrichment are free of credits, which makes them cheap to model. |

### B. Named as outside, but the evidence says reconsider

| Area | Workflows | The conflict |
|---|---|---|
| **Dialer and calls** | 15 (§11) | PRODUCT.md keeps **call tasks** inside sequences but puts the dialer outside. Apollo's KB shows these are not separable: dispositions decide whether a contact advances in a sequence, call outcomes drive contact stage triggers, "Conversations (Dialer)" feeds analytics, and parallel dialing is how a rep works a call-task queue. An Ollopa SDR with call tasks and no way to complete them is a dead end. **This is the sharpest conflict in the list.** |
| **Meetings and scheduling** | 8 (§12) | PRODUCT.md excludes meetings booking, yet "books meetings" is the SDR's job description in the roles table, and Apollo's `Meeting booked` is a contact-stage trigger, a sequence-ruleset finish condition and a workflow trigger. Ollopa can keep the *booking page* out but still needs the *meeting-booked event*. |
| **Conversations** | 11 (§13) | Recording, transcripts, trackers, scorecards and coaching are a whole product. But two pieces leak inward: AI follow-up emails and **auto-populating CRM fields from a recording** both write to records Ollopa owns. |
| **Chrome extension** | 7 (§7) | Outside by omission rather than by statement. Apollo's KB makes it the primary surface for LinkedIn prospecting and for Gmail — which means an Ollopa SDR's real day partly happens somewhere Ollopa does not exist. |

### C. Developer surfaces the boundary is silent on

| Area | Workflows | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **API** | 4 (§21) | The settings inventory already lists **API keys** and **webhooks** under Integrations, so the *keys* are inside while the *endpoints* are undefined. Note Apollo itself documents **no endpoints in its KB** — so there is no first-party endpoint list to copy. |
| **MCP and AI connectors** | 1 (§21, covering ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Cursor, Replit, Codex, Superhuman Go) | Entirely outside, and entirely new: every one of these shipped in 2026. Apollo's framing — *"Apollo remains your system of record, while your AI tool becomes the workspace where GTM gets done"* — is a direct challenge to a product whose whole thesis is what to show on a screen. If people do the work in Claude, the disclosure problem moves. |
| **CLI** | 1 (§21) | **A CLI exists.** `apollo auth login`, OAuth, no API key, covering search, enrichment, contacts, accounts, deals, sequences, calls, tasks and analytics, exporting JSON, CSV, YAML, JSONL or table. It has **no dedicated KB article** — it is documented only inside "Use Apollo with AI Tools" (45119679436557) and the 2026 release notes, with the real documentation off-KB. Ollopa has no CLI and PRODUCT.md never mentions one. |

### D. Inside the boundary, but thinner in Ollopa than in Apollo

These need no boundary change, only a note that Apollo goes further: **Sequences** (19 workflows — rulesets, stage triggers, priority settings, diagnostics, a Health tab and bounce guard have no Ollopa equivalent); **Email and deliverability** (18 — warmup, domain and mailbox *purchase*, lookalike domains, blocklist checks, inbox placement tests, bounce logs); **Workflows** (8 — Ollopa has agents, Apollo has a trigger/rule/action builder with 200+ Pipedream integrations, webhooks and AI agent steps); **Analytics** (8 — Ollopa has four fixed reports, Apollo has a report builder, goals with Slack alerts, and a Snowflake share); **Deals** (9 — forecast categories, multi-currency, deal files, CSV import).

### E. The three decisions this forces

1. **Does an Ollopa SDR make calls?** If yes, §11 comes in and the dialer stops being absent. If no, call tasks need an honest destination — Apollo's own model (a task whose completion is logged elsewhere) is the fallback.
2. **Where does a meeting come from?** Booking can stay out, but `meeting booked` has to exist as an event or sequences, stages and agents all lose a trigger they depend on.
3. **Is Ollopa still only a screen?** MCP and the CLI mean Apollo's answer is no. If Ollopa follows, progressive disclosure has to say something about surfaces where there is no screen to disclose on — which no case currently covers.

**Explicitly still outside, confirmed by this sweep:** paid ads, a website builder, a support desk, and billing of the customer's own customers. Apollo does none of these either — the closest it comes is website *visitor tracking* (§6), which is analytics, not a builder.
