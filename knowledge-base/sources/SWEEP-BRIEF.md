# Brief for the 2018–2026 research sweeps

Each sweep closes one gap in the knowledge base. The existing memos (01 to 07 in this folder) lean on experiments from 2000 to 2014 and on recent guidance rather than recent evidence. The sweeps look only at 2018 to 2026.

## Rules

- **Dates.** Sources from 2018 onwards only. If an older source is needed for context, name it as old.
- **Ranking.** Peer-reviewed first (CHI, CSCW, UIST, IUI, TOCHI, IJHCS, HFES, arXiv preprints from those communities), then industry research with published numbers (NN/g, Baymard, Pendo, Amplitude, Reforge, Growth.Design, vendor benchmark reports), then practitioner essays. Say which tier each source is.
- **Fetch and quote.** Use WebSearch and WebFetch. Quote verbatim where it matters. Do not work from memory. When a source cannot be fetched, say so and rank the claim lower.
- **For each finding:** the number, the setting it was measured in (product, population, sample size, duration), and whether it **confirms**, **refines**, or **overturns** a result the knowledge base already cites. Name the older result.
- **Close with two lists:** "Still unknown" (what the sweep could not find evidence for) and "Bibliography" with URLs.
- Plain English. 2500 to 4500 words. No open questions inside the memo; put questions for the owner in your reply.

## Read first

- `knowledge-base/00-core-model.md` sections 6, 7 and 8, so you know what is currently claimed.
- `RULES.md`, so you know what the rules currently lean on.
- The one older memo most related to your gap, named in your brief.
